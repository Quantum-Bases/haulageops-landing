Engineered for Bulk Logistics

General freight and courier software fail in bulk haulage. Here is how HaulageOps handles the commercial realities of heavy fleet operations.

Bulk Rate Engine

Complete rate card support for per-tonne, per-load, hourly, and fixed pricing. Supports client-specific rate cards with effective dating and automated expiry rules.

Contract Lifecycle

Manage the complex commercial agreements that underpin your rates. Built-in versioning, structured approval workflows, and automated notifications prior to rate expirations.

Digital Proof of Delivery

Drivers capture photos, upload dockets, and record signatures. Securely store all digital assets in Azure Blob Storage alongside licenses, permits, and permits.

Socket.io Live Dispatch

Dispatchers drag-and-drop jobs and view driver progress in real-time. Google Maps API supplies address autocompletes, dynamic routing, and exact distance calculations.

Invoice & Xero Integration

Manage invoice states from draft to sent, paid, and overdue. A 6-hour cron and webhooks sync transactions, payments, and client terms directly into Xero via secure OAuth2.

Chain of Responsibility (CoR)

Comply with AU/NZ regulatory requirements. Track driver fatigue, break schedules, rest periods, and maintain an immutable, detailed audit trail of all operational edits.

  • Automatic vehicle license and driver induction tracking
